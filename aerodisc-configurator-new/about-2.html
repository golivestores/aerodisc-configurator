Why we exist

It Started With
Wasted Range

Every EV leaves range on the table. Open-spoke wheels look sharp standing still, but at speed they churn the air and pull the battery down. The aero covers on the market were an afterthought — loose, generic, obviously aftermarket. So we built the one we wanted on our own cars: a cover that seals the wheel face, looks like it left the factory that way, and gives the range back.

How it works

One Disc. Thirty Faces.

Aerodisc never touches rolling diameter, so your speed and range readouts stay exactly true. Getting there takes three steps — and no trip to a shop.

STEP 01

Choose Your Fit

Pick your brand and model, then your favourite of thirty faces. We load the exact fitment for BYD, Denza, AION, NETA, Deepal, Avatr or MG.

STEP 02

Twist & Lock

Each cover seats over your factory wheel and twists into a patented lock — factory-tight, tool-light, about nine minutes a wheel.

Drive Farther

Cleaner airflow, less brake dust, more usable range. Wash it like any wheel and forget it's even there.
