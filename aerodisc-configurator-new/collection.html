Will the Aerodisc fit my car?
Yes. We make precision-fit sets for BYD, Denza, AION, NETA, Deepal, Avatr and MG. Choose your brand and model when you configure and we load the exact fitment.
How hard is the install?
Tool-light, about nine minutes per wheel. Each cover twists on over your factory wheel and locks — no shop visit needed.
Will it change my speed or range readout?
No. The Aerodisc sits over the wheel face and does not change rolling diameter, so your speedometer and range display read exactly as before — while the smoother face actually adds real-world range.
Will they stay on at highway speed?
Yes. Our patented twist-lock seats each cover factory-tight, validated well beyond legal highway speeds with zero rattle.
Can I still wash the car normally?
Absolutely. Wash, brush and pressure-rinse as usual. The sealed face actually keeps brake dust out of the barrel.
What about returns and warranty?
A 30-day test drive with a full refund, plus a 100,000 km finish warranty. If it's not for you, send it back.
