Description
Aerodisc is a high-speed wheel cover system engineered for performance. Our closed-face, low-drag profile streamlines airflow around the wheel and meaningfully cuts your drag coefficient — while our patented center-lock structure keeps every disc locked in, from city streets to highway cruising. Installs in 30 seconds. Swappable in every season. Performance and personality, on demand.
Aerodisc is engineered for standard street and highway use on Tesla OEM wheels. Not intended for track driving, racing, sustained off-road use, or speeds beyond posted limits. In a high-impact collision, the outer face is designed to release before transferring force to the structural rim — Aerodisc is a sacrificial accessory layer, not a crash-rated safety component. Final fitment must be verified against your VIN; once installed, a product is deemed accepted as properly fitting. Warranty does not cover normal wear (scratches, paint chipping, road-debris stains) or damage from track / aggressive driving.
Orders ship from our Los Angeles warehouse within 3–5 business days. Domestic ground delivery is free on orders over $1,500; expedited 2-day and overnight options are available at checkout. International orders typically arrive in 8–14 business days. Tracking is emailed once the carrier scans the package.
Unmounted, unmodified wheels in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund less return shipping. Custom-finished or color-matched orders are final sale. Defects covered by warranty are repaired or replaced at no cost — open a claim with your order number to start the process.

Performance & Efficiency

More Range.

A closed-face profile streamlines airflow around the wheel, smoothing the turbulence that exposed spokes create. The result: up to 10% more range per trip, a smoother ride at speed, and a noticeably quieter cabin — even in crosswinds.

Why Aerodisc

Engineered to Outlast the Road.

A wheel cover rated for the track, not just the street — protecting what's underneath, hiding what's already there, fitting every Tesla you can drive home, and built from materials that take the worst the road can throw at them.

Rated for the Track.

Where others cap out at street speed, Aerodisc keeps going. The patented center-lock clamps the cover to the hub itself — so it stays planted through hard cornering, heavy braking, and sustained high-speed running. Take it to the circuit, not just the commute.

360° Rim Protection.

A continuous outer ring wraps the rim's most vulnerable edge — taking curb strikes, parking-lot scrapes, gravel, and road salt so your OEM finish never does. The cover is the sacrificial layer; the wheel stays factory-fresh.

Hide What's Already There.

Already curbed your rim? Aerodisc covers existing scratches, chips, and corrosion in seconds — no refinish shop, no $400 repair, no waiting on parts. From the day it snaps on, the wheel looks new again.

Fits Every Tesla.

Engineered for Model 3, Model Y, Model S, and Model X — across 18", 19", 20", and 21" OEM wheels. One product line, validated against every factory rim Tesla ships, with a precise center-bore match (51–52 mm) for a flush, factory-tight fit.

Built from the Right Stuff.

High-impact ABS + PC composite with a UV-stabilized matte coating — the same material class used for automotive bumpers. Rated −40 °F to +120 °F, pressure-wash safe, and color-fast under direct sun.
